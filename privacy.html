Privacy policy - rclone backup

rclone backup is a private tool used only by its owner (Le Duc Anh) to copy his own files into his own Google Drive.

  • It accesses only the Google Drive files that it creates itself (scope drive.file).
  • No data is collected, sold, shared with third parties or sent anywhere other than the owner's Google Drive.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
